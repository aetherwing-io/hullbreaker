# Meridian foreground pack v1 — production provenance

Generated with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool and packed offline by
`tools/assets/pack-foreground-atlas.mjs`. Runtime requests one opaque
2048×2048 texture, `meridian-foreground-pack-v1.png`; the four 4×4 source
boards remain beside it for review and repacking only.

The production language is one colossal, coherent machine-creature: cold
oxidized teal body metal, restrained burnt-copper route armor, deep mechanical
recesses, readable load paths and sparse physically housed indicators. Painted
content supplies material and mechanism detail; scene geometry owns thickness,
silhouette, rims, contact shadows and traversal.

## Sheet roles

Source A contains sixteen materially distinct body choices: forged plate,
overlapping scutes, welded fascia, ribbed skin, oxidized route fascia, riveted
armor, battered overlaps, integrated grates, reinforcement, cable channels,
pressure ribs, vented armor, pitting, oil streaks, peeled paint and heat wear.
Only A may span a broad structural bay.

Source B contains sixteen ventilation, exhaust, inspection, conduit,
hydraulic, cable, machine-rib, hatch, coil, collar, cassette and junction
systems. Source C contains sixteen deck, gusset, girder, grate, ladder, guard,
cable, pipe, sensor, reservoir, cylinder, locker and hoist interfaces. B and C
retain their native proportions and sit inside genuine recessed geometry.

Source D contains sixteen coherent defense-response mechanisms in this exact
order: four Observe apertures and wake assemblies; two Intercept locks; two
Contain braces/sockets; three Quarantine seals, denial hardware and purge
fixtures; one Sterilize power junction; and four Scuttle overdrive, shearing,
controlled-excision and spent-response states.

## Packing and review contract

All 64 cells were visually accepted before packing. The generated manifest
records dimensions, cell mapping, source membership, one-texture residency,
non-emissive policy, review status, per-cell color means and a 32 px
defense-mechanism contrast proof. Identical painted outer frames are cropped at
runtime; full frames are reserved for real deep apertures.

Native-shaped structural and response cutouts are documented separately in
`meridian-component-atlas-v1.prompt.md`.
